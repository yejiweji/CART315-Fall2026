# Motivation Statement: Final Jam

I want to explore how constantly putting someone else's needs before your own can gradually become a way of life. I'm interested in how something that begins as an act of love and responsibility can turn into a cycle where your own needs become an afterthought. This is particularly interesting to me in the context of motherhood, where the expectation to be selfless can make it difficult to recognize when you've stopped making room for yourself. I want to investigate how this loss of self happens gradually, not necessarily through one major decision, but through the accumulation of small, repeated sacrifices. I'm also interested in challenging the idea that self sacrifice is the greatest expression of love. It's easy to believe that giving everything to someone else means you're being a better parent, partner, or person. However, I think there's an important distinction between caring for someone and completely neglecting yourself in the process. Taking care of your own needs allows you to grow as a person and be more present for the people around you. I want to explore the possibility that constantly pouring from an empty cup can actually undermine the care you're trying so hard to give.

For this project, I want to investigate how repetition can communicate this experience through gameplay. I'm interested in how a player might gradually become so accustomed to responding to someone else's needs that they stop questioning their own actions. At what point does caring become automatic? When do we stop asking ourselves what we want because we're so used to thinking about what everyone else needs? I want the player to experience this shift rather than simply be told about it. Ultimately, I want to question what it means to care for someone without losing yourself in the process. I don't want to frame motherhood or caregiving as the problem, but rather challenge the expectation that loving someone requires you to put your own life on hold. My goal is to create an experience that leaves the player thinking about the difference between being selfless and forgetting yourself entirely.
